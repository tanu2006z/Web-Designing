Which areas are at risk?

The video reports on Cyclone Arnab and the weather warnings issued by the India Meteorological Department (IMD). The cyclone is moving towards the Andhra Pradesh coast, creating concerns about heavy rainfall, strong winds and rough sea conditions. Andhra Pradesh is among the most affected areas, while Odisha and some nearby regions are also being monitored. The report highlights the need for weather alerts and safety precautions in coastal areas. People are advised to follow official updates and avoid going near the sea during dangerous weather conditions.


Impact of Cyclone Arnab
Cyclone Arnab caused severe weather conditions in several parts of eastern and southern India.

  1. Affected Areas: Andhra Pradesh, Odisha, Telangana, Chhattisgarh and parts of West Bengal.

  2. Heavy Rainfall: Several areas experienced heavy to extremely heavy rainfall, increasing the risk of flooding and waterlogging.

  3. Strong Winds: Coastal areas faced strong winds, rough sea conditions and thunderstorms.

  4. Damage and Disruption: Fallen trees, damaged electricity lines and blocked roads affected normal life in some areas.

  5. Safety Measures: Authorities issued weather warnings and advised people in vulnerable coastal areas to follow official instructions.


Conclusion
Cyclone Arnab highlights the importance of weather forecasting, disaster management and public awareness. People should remain alert and follow updates from official weather authorities.
